Materials Directory

What we accept

A complete guide to materials accepted in curbside recycling bins, at special drop-offs, or not accepted.

What you can recycle

ACCEPTED CURBSIDE
📰

Newspaper

Newspapers, newsprint inserts, and weekly papers.

📄

Office Paper

Printer paper, notebook paper, copy paper, and white documents.

✉️

Flyers & Mail

Unaddressed mail, advertising flyers, envelopes, and notices.

📖

Magazines

Glossy magazines, catalogs, and soft periodicals.

📒

Phone Books

Softcover phone directories and community books.

📦

Cardboard

Flattened corrugated boxes with tape and packing removed.

🥣

Boxboard

Cereal boxes, shoe boxes, and tissue boxes flattened.

🧴

Plastic Containers

Rinsed food tubs, bottles, and jugs (#1–#5).

🥫

Aluminum Cans

Clean aluminum beverage cans and drink tins.

⚙️

Steel & Tin Cans

Clean soup cans, pet food cans, and food tins.

🍾

Glass Bottles

Unbroken clear, green, and brown glass beverage bottles.

🫙

Glass Jars

Rinsed clean unbroken glass food and sauce jars.

🧃

Milk & Cartons

Paper cartons, milk cartons, and juice boxes rinsed.

Drop off

SPECIAL DROP-OFF
🔋

Lead-Acid Batteries

Vehicle and equipment batteries. Special hazardous drop-off.

🖥️

Electronic Waste

Screens, computers, TVs, electronics, cabling, and cords.

⚠️

Hazardous Waste

Paints, solvents, pesticides, and household chemicals.

🛢️

Propane Cylinders

Pressurized fuel tanks. Explosion risk; return to vendors.

💡

Fluorescent Bulbs

Fluorescent tubes and CFL bulbs containing mercury.

What can't go in the bin

NOT ACCEPTED
🚫

Styrofoam / Foam

Foam packaging, cups, and takeout containers not accepted.

🚫

Black Plastic

Optical sorting cannot read black carbon pigment. Place in garbage.

🚫

Scrap Metal

Large metal objects, pots, pans, and hardware not accepted in bins.
