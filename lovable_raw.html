About Tri-Com

A working yard, not a waiting room.

Tri-Com Recycling receives, sorts and bales material for households, municipalities and commercial haulers across the region. Everything that comes across the scale is graded, baled and shipped to end markets — nothing is quietly landfilled.

Every bin that goes out comes back — sorted, recovered, and back in the loop. That's the circle of recycle.

What we accept

Sorted loads move faster and pay better. Here's what belongs on the tipping floor.

Paper & cardboard

Flattened boxboard, corrugate, office paper, newsprint.

Containers

Rinsed plastics #1–#5, aluminium, steel cans, glass jars.

Metals

Household scrap, appliances with refrigerant tags removed.

Electronics

Screens, computers, small appliances and cabling.

Beverage containers

Deposit-return containers sorted by material.

Not accepted

Wet organics, medical sharps, asbestos, unlabelled drums.

Stewardship programs

Registered take-back streams handled under provincial producer-responsibility rules.

Electronics recycling

Registered depot for end-of-life consumer electronics.

Tire take-back

Passenger and light-truck tires, off the rim, no charge.

Used oil & antifreeze

Containers, filters and fluids handled under program rules.

Paint & solvents

Collected on hazardous-waste event days.

Safety & compliance

  • Stay in your vehicle until a yard attendant directs you to a bay.
  • High-visibility vest and closed-toe footwear required beyond the tipping line.
  • No open flame, smoking or idling near baled material.
  • Loads are inspected; unacceptable material is refused and documented.
  • Incidents and spills are reported to the provincial regulator within 24 hours.
