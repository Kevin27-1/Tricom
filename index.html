Three communities. One facility.

Tri-Com Recycling is jointly governed by the Town of The Pas, Opaskwayak Cree Nation, and the Rural Municipality of Kelsey, with support from Multi-Material Stewardship Manitoba. One facility, built and run together.

Tri-Com operates within and serves Treaty 5 Territory, in partnership with Opaskwayak Cree Nation.

REGULATORY STANDARDS

Safety & Compliance

Tri-Com operates under Manitoba's workplace safety, hazardous materials, and privacy regulations, including WHMIS 2015 and the Transportation of Dangerous Goods Act. We're committed to full regulatory compliance as the facility completes its phased reopening.

Governing Legislative Frameworks

Manitoba Workplace Safety and Health Act (C.C.S.M. c. W210) & Regulation MR 217/2006 WHMIS 2015 Transportation of Dangerous Goods Act, 1992 (TDG) Manitoba Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act (FIPPA) Manitoba Waste Reduction and Prevention (WRAP) Act
